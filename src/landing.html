How it works

From a number to a full itinerary in a minute.

  1. Set your budget

    Pick a destination, your dates, a daily budget and what you're into: food, outdoors, nightlife or family stuff.

  2. Get your plan

    Each day comes with times, prices, addresses and who to contact. Swap anything and the totals update.

  3. Keep saving

    Forward your hotel, flight or car confirmations. When prices drop or a promo appears, we show you how to rebook.
